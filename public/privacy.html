Privacy Policy

WordLift AI Audit · Effective 6 September 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains what WordLift AI Audit collects, why, who sees it, and how long it is kept. It supplements the WordLift privacy policy, which covers wordlift.io and WordLift's other products. Where the two differ for this service, this page applies.

In short
  • The service needs no account. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party scripts.
  • A basic audit collects one thing from you: the address of the website to audit.
  • A deep scan also asks for an email address, so the finished report can be sent to you.
  • Reports are public documents: anyone with the link can read them. They are deleted automatically 30 days after they are created.
  • We never sell personal data, and we show no advertising.

3.2 Information about the website you audit

An audit reads public pages of the site you name: four representative pages for a basic scan, up to twelve for a deep scan, together with the site's structured data and the discovery documents it publishes (for example under /.well-known/). Where the site declares a search or agent interface, the audit may invoke it to verify that it works. Pages are fetched the way a browser would, without cookies or credentials, and the audit identifies itself with a fixed user agent.

What we keep is evidence, not pages: a normalized description of what the site offers, with at most 100 evidence items of at most 500 characters each. Raw HTML is discarded as soon as the evidence is extracted and is never stored.

That evidence can incidentally include personal data the site itself publishes, such as the names or contact details on a team or contact page. We treat it as the public information it is, keep it only within the report, and delete it with the report. If you run a site and want a report about it removed, see section 9.

3.3 Technical data

  • Your IP address, used to limit how many audits one address can start in a ten-minute window. Counters live in memory and are not written anywhere.
  • Server logs: the request path, status, timing, the client's IP address and user agent, and error names. Logs contain neither the email address a deep scan was sent to nor the content of audited pages. Logs are kept for 30 days.

3.4 When you use the service through an AI assistant

If you reach the audit through ChatGPT, Claude, or another MCP client, that provider handles your conversation under its own privacy policy. We receive only the inputs of the tool being called: the URL, the depth, the email address if you gave one, a report id, a claim token, or the refinement you confirmed. Requests from hosted assistants arrive from the provider's servers, so the IP address we see is the provider's, not yours. We record which surface a deep scan came from (web form, in-page agent, or MCP server) so we can tell the sources apart; we do not identify you.

5. Deep scans and the report email

A deep scan is the one exchange in the service: a deeper read of the site for an email address, which is where the finished report is sent.

When the report completes, we submit your address to WordLift's customer platform, HubSpot, together with the audited URL, the readiness score and a link to the report. HubSpot sends you the report on our behalf and records you as a contact of WordLift. WordLift may follow up about your audit results and the WordLift services that address them. Every such email carries an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete the contact at any time (section 9).

The address is used for nothing else. It is never written into the report, never appears in a shared link, and is shown masked wherever the service reads it back.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, delete, or restrict the personal data we hold about you, receive it in a portable format, object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was done before. Write to privacy@wordlift.io. To act on a report, include its link. We answer within one month.

If you own a website that was audited and want its report removed, tell us the report link and the site; we will delete the report and everything stored with it. If you want the report corrected instead, refining it through the service creates a new report that records your judgment alongside the machine's draft.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority. In Italy that is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali; elsewhere in the EEA, the authority of your country.

10. Security

The service is reachable over HTTPS only. Every address it fetches passes a policy that refuses private, internal and reserved networks, so an audit cannot be turned into a probe of the infrastructure it runs on. No cookies, credentials or caller headers are ever forwarded to an audited site. Provider credentials stay on the server in a secrets manager and never reach the browser. Claim tokens are stored only as hashes. The service is open source, so anyone can check these statements against the code. Report vulnerabilities to security@wordlift.io.

11. Cookies and tracking

The service sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or social-media scripts. Fonts are served from our own domain. Nothing is stored in your browser between visits.

12. Children

The service is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
