Privacy Policy

WordLift AI Audit · Effective 27 September 2026 · Version 1.1

This policy explains what WordLift AI Audit collects, what each tool can return, why the data is used, who receives it, how long WordLift keeps it, and what controls are available to you. It supplements the WordLift privacy policy. Where the two differ for this service, this page applies.

In short
  • The service needs no WordLift account. It sets no cookies and runs no advertising or analytics scripts.
  • A basic audit needs a public website URL. A deep scan also needs an email address so the report can be delivered.
  • Audit and refinement reports are public-by-link documents and are automatically deleted from WordLift storage after 30 days.
  • When you use the remote MCP server through ChatGPT, Claude, or another assistant, the inputs selected for a tool call are sent to WordLift and the tool result is sent back to that assistant provider as part of your interaction.
  • We do not sell personal data. WordLift does not use personal data from this service to train machine-learning models.

3.2 Public information collected from the website you ask us to audit

An audit reads public pages of the site you name: four representative pages for a basic scan, up to twelve for a deep scan, plus public structured data and discovery documents (for example files under /.well-known/). Where a site declares a search or agent interface, the audit may invoke it to verify that it works. Pages are fetched without your cookies or credentials.

WordLift stores normalized evidence rather than raw pages: a compact description of the site's content, entities, capabilities and technical findings, including short evidence snippets. Raw HTML is discarded after evidence extraction and is not stored in the report store.

Public page content can incidentally contain personal data that the audited site has published, such as a person's name or business contact details. That information is used only as audit evidence, kept within the report, and deleted when the report expires or is removed.

3.3 Technical and security data

  • IP address and user agent, used for abuse prevention, rate limiting and server logs.
  • Request path, status, timing and error name in server logs. Logs do not contain deep-scan email addresses or raw audited page content.
  • Which surface initiated a deep scan (web, webmcp, or mcp) so we can distinguish delivery sources.

Rate-limit counters live in memory for about ten minutes. Server logs are retained for 30 days.

3.5 When you use an AI assistant or MCP client

The assistant provider decides which explicit tool inputs to send to WordLift. WordLift receives only the fields of that tool call, not your full conversation history. After the tool runs, WordLift returns the tool result to the calling client. The assistant provider therefore receives the result categories listed in section 3.4 and may use them as context in your conversation.

For example, when ChatGPT calls WordLift AI Audit, OpenAI receives the tool result back from WordLift; when Claude calls it, Anthropic receives the result. Those providers process your conversation and the returned tool data under their own terms, privacy policies and account/workspace controls. WordLift does not control how long an assistant provider retains your conversation or whether your account settings allow that provider to use conversation content for model improvement.

Requests from hosted assistants normally reach WordLift from the provider's infrastructure, so the IP address WordLift sees can be the provider's egress address rather than your device address.

6. Deep scans, email and follow-up

A basic scan does not require an email address. A deep scan requires an address so the finished report can be delivered. When the report completes, WordLift submits that address to HubSpot together with the audited URL, readiness score, report link and source surface. HubSpot sends the report on WordLift's behalf and stores the address as a WordLift contact.

WordLift may follow up about the audit findings and WordLift services relevant to them. You can withdraw consent or unsubscribe at any time. The email address is never written into the public audit report or returned in a report link, and service responses that read it back mask the address.

7. International transfers

WordLift's current production configuration stores reports, deep-scan addresses and logs in Google Cloud in the United States. Where EEA personal data is transferred outside the EEA, WordLift relies on applicable transfer mechanisms such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified recipients and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in provider data-processing terms. ScrapingBee processes its rendering service in the European Union. AI assistant providers process returned tool data according to their own privacy terms and data-residency arrangements.

9. Your controls and rights

You can limit what you share with WordLift AI Audit by choosing the workflow that fits your request:

  • Use a basic scan if you do not want to provide an email address.
  • Do not include personal data in refinement text unless you want it published in the resulting report.
  • Use the WordLift website directly instead of an AI assistant if you do not want the tool result returned into an assistant conversation.
  • Use your assistant provider's controls to manage or delete conversations and to manage any model-improvement setting the provider offers.
  • Contact WordLift to request deletion of a report or personal data held by WordLift.

Under the GDPR, where applicable, you can ask WordLift to access, correct, delete or restrict personal data, receive it in a portable format, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Write to privacy@wordlift.io. To act on a report, include its URL. We normally answer within one month.

If you own a website that was audited and want its report removed, send us the report link and site URL. We will delete the report and data stored with it from WordLift's report store. Copies already retained by a third-party assistant, search engine or recipient of a shared link must be handled with that third party.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority. In Italy that is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

10. Security

The service is reachable over HTTPS. URLs fetched by the audit pass a policy that rejects private, internal and reserved network destinations. WordLift does not forward your browser cookies, credentials or caller headers to audited sites. Provider credentials remain server-side in secret storage. Claim tokens are stored by WordLift only as hashes. Security reports can be sent to security@wordlift.io.

11. Cookies and tracking

The WordLift AI Audit website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or social-media scripts. Fonts are served from our own domain. Server-side operational logging is described in sections 3.3 and 8.
